## 1. Overview

Foreign-invested enterprises account for the larger part of Vietnam's exports, yet two questions are usually studied apart: what draws FDI into Vietnam, and how FDI turns into trade. This programme links them in one chain:

**cross-national distance (CAGE) → FDI inflows into Vietnam → exports and imported inputs of the FDI sector**

It builds on two earlier studies:

- Phan and Đỗ (2019) estimated a gravity model of FDI inflows from 16 source countries, 2006–2015.
- Xuan and Xing (2008) showed that FDI raised Vietnam's exports to source countries, 1990–2004, and noted that registered FDI is a poor measure of realised investment.

The programme updates both with longer data, current estimators (PPML, local projections, event-study difference-in-differences) and a monthly series of FDI-sector trade from Vietnam Customs, 2013–2026.

## 5. Data policy

- **Public sources** (World Bank WDI, Worldwide Governance Indicators, CEPII GeoDist, Hofstede scores, WTO RTA Database, IMF CDIS, UN Comtrade, OECD TiVA, published Customs and FDI releases) are cited with access dates. Derived variables are shared.
- **Purchased data.** The consolidated Customs series for 2013–2026 was purchased from a commercial provider. Raw files are stored in a private component and are shared only as far as the licence allows. The codebook, concordances, cleaning log, analysis code and all aggregated results are public.
- **Human participants (DA4).** No data are collected before ethics approval and informed consent. Participants are identified only by hashed team codes, and no personal identifiers are stored.

## 6. Open teaching tools

These tools apply the programme's data and methods in the classroom. They run in the browser, work offline and send no data off the user's device.

- **Gravity Lab**: re-reads the published gravity results, simulates effects, contrasts log-OLS with PPML, and estimates OLS/PPML on a user's own CSV. https://thuyhuongctu.github.io/BizOn/lab/gravity-lab.html
- **FDI Factory (Nhà máy FDI)**: a single-player simulation of a foreign-invested plant over eight quarters of trade shocks, sourcing choices and rules of origin. https://thuyhuongctu.github.io/BizOn/lab/nha-may-fdi.html

Both are part of the BizOn ecosystem (archived on Zenodo: [10.5281/zenodo.21592241](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21592241)). They are proprietary software and are linked here, not deposited.
