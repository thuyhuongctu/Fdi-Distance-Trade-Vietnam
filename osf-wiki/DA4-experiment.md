# DA4 · Distance and Entry-Mode Choice: An Experiment

> *Tóm tắt:* Thực nghiệm có kiểm soát trên nền tảng mô phỏng BizOn, kiểm tra xem người ra quyết định có điều chỉnh lựa chọn thị trường và phương thức thâm nhập theo khoảng cách văn hóa và thể chế hay không. Đây là nền tảng vi mô cho kết quả vĩ mô của DA1. Chưa thu thập dữ liệu cho đến khi được phê duyệt đạo đức.

**Part of:** [Distance, FDI and the Trade of Foreign-Invested Firms in Vietnam](../) · **Status:** Design stage, subject to ethics approval · **Target journal:** *International Business Review*

## Motivation

At the country level, Phan and Đỗ (2019) found no significant effect of cultural or institutional distance on FDI into Vietnam. DA4 asks whether distance matters at the level of individual decisions, where macro data cannot see it.

## Planned design

- **Platform:** the *Brand Passport* and *Go Global* simulations in BizOn, whose deterministic engines hold all other conditions fixed.
- **Manipulation:** the cultural and institutional distance of the simulated markets is varied systematically along CAGE dimensions.
- **Outcomes:** market choice, entry mode (export, licensing, joint venture, greenfield FDI), and purchase of market intelligence.
- **Analysis:** conditional logit models of market and entry-mode choice.
- **Participants:** university students, with MBA students or managers as a planned extension to address external validity.

## Ethics and consent

- No data are collected before approval by the institutional ethics committee and an approved consent form.
- BizOn's decision-log consent gate stays **off** by default. Participants are identified only by hashed team codes, and no names or emails are stored.
